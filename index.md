---
title: Mealometry Privacy Policy
---


Mealometry stores your food log, goals and settings on your device. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we don't collect, sell or share personal information.

**iCloud backup.** If you're signed in to iCloud, Mealometry saves a backup of your data in the app's private iCloud storage so you can restore it on a new phone. This backup lives in your own iCloud account. We can't see it.

**Food lookups.** When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search words or barcode number are sent to USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts to find nutrition information. These requests aren't linked to your name or account.

**Camera.** The camera is used only to read barcodes. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

**Estimates, not medical advice.** Nutrition data comes from public databases and may contain errors. Goal suggestions are general estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian for personal guidance.
